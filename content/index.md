## About

My name is Fengtao Wan, and I am a third-year PhD student in Marketing at the [UCL School of Management](https://www.mgmt.ucl.ac.uk/), supervised by [Yiting Deng](https://www.yiting-deng.com) and [Wei Miao](https://miaowei.netlify.app/). My research focuses on **digital platforms**, using applied econometrics to understand consumer behavior in digital environments. Before joining UCL, I completed an MPhil in [Business Data Science](https://businessdatascience.nl/home) and a Bachelor’s in Finance at [Lingnan College, Sun Yat-sen University](https://lingnan.sysu.edu.cn/en/introduction).
